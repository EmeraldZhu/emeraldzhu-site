### What is mTenure? 

A property management system (PMS) that helps landlords to: 

- do their monthly billings
- issue statements to tenants
- send reminders to tenants
- record payments from tenants
- track profits and arrears
- track business expenses
- draft and send leases to your tenants
- download your reports anytime
