### So what exactly is Arxidian?

No, it's not a typo for Arcadian (blame Google). Jokes aside, Arxidian is a secure escrow and transaction verification platform. Longer version, Arxidian serves as a secure third-party intermediary for buying and selling high-value assets *(anything from high-end electronics to cars, land and more, basically, involve us wherever money is exchanging hands)*. 

### How does Arxidian work?

> ### **Buyer's POV**

You've seen an item on an online marketplace, say Jiji, for example. You have the money to buy, but you don't know the seller. You start thinking to yourself: 

- Is the seller legit?
- Is the item actually genuine? 
- What if what's listed isn't what I get?

These questions and doubts are normal for any buyer about to part ways with their money in exchange for an item. We make the experience less stressful and establish trust between you and the seller you're about to buy from. 

> **How, you might ask?** 

Quite simple actually. You reach out to us, you show us the item and seller you've identified. We hold the buyer's funds securely in a lawyer/attorney escrow account. *(Why don't we hold the money ourselves? Clients' money isn't our operating/personal fund)*. We reach out to the seller, and if needed, conduct item testing and verification *(non-tangible services are also covered)*. We then document and draft contractual obligations between the buyer and seller. Once all contractual conditions are met, funds are released to the seller after the buyer receives the item. 

Convinced? [Buy with us](https://www.arxidian.com/)

> ### **Seller's POV**

So you're a seller. "What's in it for me?" you might ask. Well... 

- We, Arxidian, verify your items thus increasing buyers' trust in you/the product/service.
- No meeting with strangers. Customer inquiries are from real and serious buyers, since their funds are held in escrow.

Convinced? [Sell with us](https://www.arxidian.com/sell/)
